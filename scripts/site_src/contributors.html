The people behind DocsForge.

DocsForge is open source and MIT-licensed. Every bug report, fix and idea changes how AI agents get their documentation, and everyone who has shipped code is listed here.

Become a contributor

No contribution is too small. Bug fixes and documentation count as much as new crawling strategies.

Report a problem

A bug, a bad extraction, or a library resolved to the wrong documentation. Say which tool you called, what you gave it, and what came back.

Fix or improve something

Crawling and scraping, documentation extraction, the MCP tools, the site, the tests. Pick an open issue or bring your own.

Propose a bigger change

A new source type or crawling strategy, a new tool, a new integration. The contributing guide says how to get in touch.
